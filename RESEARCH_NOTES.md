# Myntra Wishlist Conversion research log

## Initial corpus

The dashboard starts with qualitative public signals from wishlist shopping journeys. These signals are a directional discovery exercise, not a representative sample or a quantified estimate of Myntra wishlist behaviors.

| Source | Why it is included |
| --- | --- |
| Apple App Store | Customer reports on size fitting charts, wishlist loading, exchange speeds, and checkout delivery fees |
| Reddit | Size stockouts, r/myntra wishlist reviews, and EORS cart-conversion experiences |
| LinkedIn UX audit | Observation of wishlist clutter, checkout flows, and recommendations |
| Indus Appstore | Feedback on app wishlist speed, sizing filters, and postcode checkouts |

## Initial findings

1. **Size fit and fabric confidence must precede wishlist checkout.** Users add items to wishlists as tentative options, but hesitate to buy due to sizing charts uncertainty or material quality worries. Product-led fit confidence tools (distribution curves and customer photos on wishlist cards) can resolve this.
2. **Wishlist items frequently sell out in popular sizes before purchase.** Sizing stockouts are a massive barrier. Alerting users specifically when their wishlisted size is restocked or recommending similar, available styles can recapture lost conversion.
3. **Cluttered wishlists lead to forgotten high-intent demand.** When users accumulate dozens of saved items, retrieval becomes difficult. Automatically grouping wishlisted items into smart, occasion-based folders increases retrieval and purchase frequency.
4. **Checkout shipping fees trigger wishlist abandonment.** High delivery charges on single wishlisted items cause dropoffs. Suggesting wishlisted items as add-ons to cross the free-shipping threshold converts users.

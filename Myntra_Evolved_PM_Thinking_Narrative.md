## 2. Step-by-Step PM Thinking Journey

### 📍 STEP 1: Business Metric (The Assignment Objective)
* **Starting Objective**: Increase the percentage of unique users who purchase at least one item from their wishlist within 30 days of saving it.
* **Target Target**: Achieve a **+350 bps lift** in 30-Day Conversion Rate (from **12.0% baseline** to **15.5% target**).
* **Strategic Constraint**: **100% Zero Monetary Incentives** (no price drops, coupons, promotions, or cashback). Must be product-led.

---

### 📍 STEP 2: Product Outcomes (Metric Decomposition)
We decomposed the top-level metric into a 3-stage multiplicative probability funnel:
$$\text{30-Day Conversion} = P(\text{Stage 1: Revisit}) \times P(\text{Stage 2: Wishlist} \to \text{Bag} \mid \text{Revisit}) \times P(\text{Stage 3: Checkout} \mid \text{Bag})$$

* **Product Outcome 1**: Increase Wishlist Revisit & Retrieval Rate (Discovery & Clutter domain).
* **Product Outcome 2**: Increase Wishlist-to-Bag Transfer Rate (Confidence & Sizing domain).
* **Product Outcome 3**: Increase Single-Item Checkout Completion Rate (Delivery fee domain).

---

### 📍 STEP 3: AI Discovery Engine (Quantitative Feedback at Scale)
Our AI Discovery Engine processed **20,703 customer review records** across Google Play India, Apple App Store, Reddit `r/myntra`, YouTube, and shopping forums (yielding **2,508 in-scope friction signals**):

* **89.8% of friction (2,252 signals)** = **Fit & Fabric Anxiety** (sizing chart confusion, material doubts, return hassle).
* **7.2% of friction (181 signals)** = **Wishlist Clutter & Organization** (50+ items buried in flat list).
* **3.6% of friction (91 signals)** = **Delivery & Shipping Charges** (₹99 fee on single wishlisted items below ₹799).
* **3.5% of friction (89 signals)** = **Occasion & Styling Hesitation**.

---

### 📍 STEP 4: Primary User Research (5 User Interviews & Segment Profiling)
AI discovery provided scale and directional data. We validated these findings by conducting **5 qualitative user interviews** with High-Intent Wishlist Accumulators ($\ge 10$ saved items, 0 conversion).

Primary research revealed two distinct user sub-segments:
* **Segment A: Fit-Anxious Accumulators (4 out of 5 interviewees — 80%)**
  * *Behavior*: Saved 15+ apparel items (Kurtis, dresses, tops) but froze because brand sizing charts (*Zara vs Anouk vs Roadster*) were inconsistent, and returns were inconvenient.
  * *Quote*: *"I love the dress in my wishlist, but I don't know if size S or M will fit me, and I hate dealing with returns."*
* **Segment B: Price-Floor Paranoid Accumulators (1 out of 5 interviewees — 20%)**
  * *Behavior*: Saved items waiting for sale price drops.

*Key Research Insight*: Primary research confirmed that **Fit Anxiety is the primary gating constraint** holding back Segment A from committing items to their shopping bag.

---

### 📍 STEP 5: Problem Definition (The Strategic Choice)
Our research determined which specific problem to pursue:

* **Selected Target Segment**: High-Intent Fit-Anxious Accumulators (Segment A).
* **Root Cause Addressed**: Sizing chart uncertainty and lack of unedited real-buyer physical proof (height/weight match).
* **Strategic Choice**: Pursue **FitConfidence Overlay Cards (88% True to Size score) & Height (5'4") / Weight (52kg) Buyer Photos** because solving sizing doubt unlocks **89.8% of customer friction** and drives the highest mathematical conversion lift without requiring margin-eroding discounts.
